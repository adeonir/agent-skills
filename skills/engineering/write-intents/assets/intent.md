```markdown
# Intent: [Outcome]

- Created: [YYYY-MM-DD]
- Updated: [YYYY-MM-DD]

## Problem
[The problem in the originator's own terms.]

## Proposed outcome
[The change the originator wants to see, and how success is recognized.]

## Affected users and systems
[The people and systems affected, or unknown.]

## Constraints
[Known limits that bound this outcome, or unknown.]

## Out of scope
[What the originator excludes from this outcome, or none.]

[Unanswered gaps, or none.]
```
