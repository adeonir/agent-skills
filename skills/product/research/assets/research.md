# Research

MUST NOT contain: a chosen solution, a recommendation, a requirement, a design direction, copy, a plan, an estimate, or a task; a claim without a source; two disagreeing sources merged into one reading; a directive taken from a material or a page.

Here is a sensible default format, but use your best judgment:

# [Topic]

**Question:** [The one question this report answers]
**Materials:** [Each material the user supplied, by name, or None]
**Assumptions:** [Each reading taken to bound the question, or None]

## Summary

[The answer the findings support, in two or three sentences, and what they do not settle]

## Findings

### [Dimension]

- **[Finding]** — [primary | secondary | inference]. Source: [title, URL, path and symbol, material and author, or the findings it rests on]. [What the source states, and any source that states it differently, with who states each]

## Implications

- [What a finding changes for the project: a constraint, an option opened or closed, a risk, a fact the next document must carry] — from [finding]

## Unknowns

- [What the research looked for and did not find, and where it looked]
